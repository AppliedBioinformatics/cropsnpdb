About

The Brassica single nucleotide polymorphism database (Brassica SNPdb) is a project initiated at a meeting of the Multinational Brassica Genome project during the Brassica 2016 conference to help integrate SNP array data in an open community platform. The MySQL database hosts SNP array data collected using the Brassica 60k Illumina Infinium genotyping arrays. The database has been recently expanded to include data from the Wheat iSelect 90k array, and thus renamed Crop SNPdb. Researchers who have collected Brassica or wheat genotyping array data are encouraged to upload their data and share it with the community via this open platform.

Contact information: Dave.Edwards@uwa.edu.au
